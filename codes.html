Public codes that I (co-)develop

Dusty FARGO-ADSG

Branch of the original FARGO 2D hydrocode that includes gas self-gravity, an energy equation with various source terms, and dust modelled as Lagrangian super-particles and/or a pressureless fluid. [Link to github]

FARGO2PYTHON

A Python program to visualize the outputs of the original FARGO code, of the Dusty FARGO-ADSG and FARGO-3D codes. [Link to github]

FARGO2RADMC3D

A Python program that computes synthetic images of the gas and/or dust emission of a protoplanetary disc via RADMC3D from the results of Dusty FARGO-ADSG or FARGO-3D hydrodynamical simulations. [Link to github]

Postvecp2python

A Python program to visualise the results of linear hydrodynamical simulations modelling oscillation modes in stars and planets with the LSB spectral code. [Link to github]
