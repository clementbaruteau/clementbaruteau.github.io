# clementbaruteau.github.io

Professional homepage of Clément Baruteau (CNRS / IRAP, University of Toulouse)

Welcome! I am a CNRS researcher at the Institute for Research in Astrophysics and Planetology, University of Toulouse. My main research interests are in the formation and evolution of planetary systems. I specialize in the study of the interactions between planets, the disc of gas and dust where they form, and their star. My work aims at understanding the various structures observed in protoplanetary discs, the architecture of the Solar System and of the many extrasolar planetary systems.
